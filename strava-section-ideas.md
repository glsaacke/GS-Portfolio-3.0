# Strava Section — Design Ideas

## ✅ Option A: "Beyond the Code" section — right after Intro (currently implementing)

Move the Strava section directly under the Intro with a framing headline and short personal blurb.

**Headline:** "Beyond the Code"  
**Blurb:** Something like — *"When I'm not building software, I'm usually outside on a bike or on the track. Here's what I've been up to lately:"*  
**Placement:** Between `<Intro/>` and `<Experience/>` in `Home.jsx`

Gives it immediate context — a visitor learns something personal about you before even seeing your work history.

---

## Option B: Personality stat bar in the Intro itself

Add a small row of "fun facts" directly in the Intro section — something like:

> 🚴 Cyclist · 🏃 Track athlete · 📍 Birmingham, AL

...with the Strava card as a live expandable or a subtle "Latest ride →" link inline. Keeps it very lightweight and immediately visible without taking up its own section.

---

## Option C: "About Me" interlude section (most storytelling impact)

A dedicated short section between Intro and Experience that acts like a personality snapshot — a few interest chips/icons (cycling, track, etc.) alongside the live Strava card. Frames it as: *"here's the full person, not just the resume."* Could include other hobbies or fun stats beyond just Strava.
